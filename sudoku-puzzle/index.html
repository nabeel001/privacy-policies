Privacy Policy for Sudoku Puzzle

Effective date: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Noble Technologies ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Sudoku Puzzle mobile application (the "App"), available on the Google Play Store.

1. Summary

Sudoku Puzzle does not require an account and does not collect any personal information you type or enter. All puzzle generation, gameplay, statistics, and streak tracking happen entirely on your device and work offline. The App offers an optional daily reminder notification, which is scheduled locally on your device. The only data collection on this App comes from the third-party advertising service (Google AdMob) used to display ads, described below.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require registration, sign-in, or any account creation.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, or precise location.
  • All gameplay data — puzzles played, completion times, mistake and hint counts, game history, daily puzzle streaks, streak calendar records, and settings — is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to us or to any server we control.
  • The daily puzzle is generated using a shared, date-based method so that all players receive the same puzzle on a given day; this does not involve sending any of your personal or device information to a server.

3. Notifications

The App offers an optional daily reminder notification that lets you know when a new daily puzzle is ready. This feature:

  • Is off by default and only activates if you turn it on in Settings, or opt in when prompted after solving a daily puzzle.
  • Is scheduled entirely on your device using the Android operating system's local notification and alarm scheduling tools — no notification content or scheduling data is sent to us or to any third party.
  • Is automatically skipped on days you've already solved the daily puzzle.
  • May request the device's notification permission (and, on some Android versions, permission to run after a device restart, so reminders continue working) — these permissions are used solely to deliver this feature and are not used to collect or share personal information.

You can disable this feature at any time in Settings, or by revoking the App's notification permission in your device's system settings.

4. Information Collected by Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements — including banner ads and full-screen (interstitial) ads — through Google AdMob, and offers optional rewarded video ads that unlock extra undos, extra hints, or a "second chance" after running out of mistakes. To serve and measure these ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect certain information from your device, which may include:

  • Advertising identifier (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, and general region)
  • App usage and interaction data related to ads (such as ad views, clicks, and rewarded ad completions)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (not precise GPS location)

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own privacy policy, not by us directly. You can review how Google collects and uses this data here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In regions where the law requires it (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, and other jurisdictions with similar requirements), the App presents Google's consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before showing personalized ads, allowing you to choose whether to consent to personalized advertising and the associated data processing. You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time from the "ad privacy options" link in Settings.

We do not sell your personal information. Where applicable privacy laws (such as the CCPA) give you rights over information collected through advertising on the App, those requests are handled through the ad privacy options described above or by contacting us using the details in Section 10.

You can also opt out of personalized advertising, or reset your advertising identifier, through your device's system settings (on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads, wording may vary by device and Android version).

5. Children's Privacy

Sudoku Puzzle is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

6. Data Retention

Since gameplay data, streak information, and notification preferences are stored only on your device, they persist only until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. We do not retain any copy of this data ourselves, as we never receive it.

7. Data Security

Because no personal or gameplay data is transmitted to or stored by us, there is no server-side data of yours for us to secure. Data collected by Google AdMob is secured according to Google's own security practices.

8. Your Choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time via your device settings.
  • You can turn the daily reminder notification on or off at any time in Settings, or by changing the App's notification permission in your device settings.
  • You can review or withdraw your advertising consent at any time via the ad privacy options link in Settings.
  • You can uninstall the App at any time, which removes all locally stored gameplay data, streaks, and preferences from your device.
  • You can opt out of certain interest-based advertising via Google Ads Settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or in applicable law. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Privacy Policy for FitCalc: Fitness Check

Last updated: 29 August 2026

FitCalc: Fitness Check ("the app") is developed by Noble Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. We have tried to keep it specific rather than generic, so you can tell exactly what happens to your data.

We do not collect personal information

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no registration. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number, and we operate no servers that receive your information.

Your measurements stay on your device

The height, weight, age, gender, and body measurements you enter are used only to perform the calculation you asked for, on your device, at that moment. They are never transmitted anywhere, and never saved — closing the app discards them.

What is stored on your device

The app saves exactly one setting locally: whether you enabled dark mode. It stays on your device, is not readable by us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app displays ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect and process certain information, including your device's advertising identifier, device and operating system details, general location inferred from your IP address, and data about ad interactions.

This processing is carried out by Google, not by us. We never receive your individual data — only aggregate reporting such as total impressions. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy & Terms and described in How Google uses information from AdMob.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before any personalised advertising is shown, and you may change or withdraw that choice at any time.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads on your Android device.
  • Opt out of ad personalisation in the same Android settings screen.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the dark mode setting, the only data it stores.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them. Body composition results are intended for adults; they are not clinically meaningful for children or adolescents.

Not medical advice

BMI and body fat figures are general fitness estimates and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
